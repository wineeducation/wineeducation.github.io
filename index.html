About

The wine industry doesn't teach consumers what they really need to know so it can seem like there is so much to learn and memorize. It's why getting into wine can be discouraging, and that's what I've set out to change.

In just a couple of hours, I teach the essentials and demystify the rest. Best of all, I come to you so you can learn about wine in your own space with those you want there with you.

Details

- a single two hour event
